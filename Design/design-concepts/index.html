Ten designs for this site

Someone told me my site looks like a default Microsoft template. They had a point. So I've mocked up ten different directions, and I'd like your opinion on which one I should go with.

Each design is a working home page with my real content. Try a few on your phone as well as a computer, and in dark mode if you use it. The bar at the bottom of each design takes you to the next one.

The forms in the designs don't send anything. Use the form at the bottom of this page to tell me what you think.
